Lake District, 1994

The Silence of the Morning

There is a specific kind of quiet that only exists before the rest of the world wakes. It feels like the air is heavy with unwritten stories. The fog today was particularly thick, clinging to the valley floors like a heavy wool blanket. I sat for hours just watching the light change from steel gray to a warm, bruised purple.

Looking back at these moments, I realize they aren't just memories—they are the threads that hold my sense of self together.
