📖 SheCare Memory Diary – Product Specification
Overview

Design a premium digital diary that feels like writing inside a beautiful physical notebook rather than using a social media app or a notes app.

The diary should be emotional, personal, elegant, and distraction-free.

Users create memories by freely arranging text, photos, videos, voice notes, and stickers anywhere on a notebook page.

The experience should resemble a handcrafted scrapbook with realistic page-turning interactions.

Overall Experience

The user should feel like they are opening a real memory book, not creating a document. Every page should feel unique because users decide where to place their memories, photos, videos, and notes. Luna quietly accompanies them throughout the experience, adding warmth without interrupting the creative process.

One UX recommendation

I would make the diary have two modes:

📖 Read Mode (Default)
Clean notebook with no editing controls.
Swipe between pages.
Tap photos to view them.
Tap videos to play them.
Luna is visible.
✏️ Edit Mode
Triggered by an Edit button.
Objects become draggable and resizable.
Floating toolbar appears.
Luna moves to a small corner and stops interacting to avoid interfering with editing.

This separation makes the diary feel like a real book when reading memories and a creative workspace only when the user intentionally chooses to edit. It's a cleaner and less overwhelming experience than always showing editing handles and controls.
